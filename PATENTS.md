# Patent promise

**Fluxcode Studio LLC — dated 2026-09-17.**

To encourage adoption of the `.crate` format, Fluxcode Studio LLC makes the following promise
to every person, company and organization that implements it.

## The promise

To the extent Fluxcode Studio LLC owns or controls patent claims that are necessarily infringed
by an implementation that conforms to the `.crate` format specification as published in
[FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md) (the "Required Claims"), Fluxcode Studio LLC **irrevocably promises**
that it will never assert those Required Claims against you — for making, having made, using,
selling, offering for sale, importing, or distributing such an implementation — royalty-free,
worldwide, forever. This promise applies to every revision of the specification published in
this repository, including future revisions, for as long as Fluxcode Studio LLC stewards the
format, and it binds any successor steward that acquires those claims.

## Defensive termination

If you, or a company you control, file a lawsuit (or a direct claim in a proceeding) alleging
that a conformant implementation of the `.crate` format infringes a patent, the promise above
is withdrawn as to you for that patent. Everyone else keeps it.

## No other rights

This promise grants no rights other than the patent promise stated here — no rights under
copyright (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), **no trademark rights** (Crate and the crate logo are marks
of Fluxcode Studio LLC; conformant implementations may truthfully state that they read or write
the `.crate` format), and no rights by implication, waiver or estoppel. This promise is not an
assurance that any implementation is free of third-party patents; we state our own promise
plainly because that is the part we can control.

## Why this file exists

A format that others may implement deserves more than a copyright license: copyright covers the
*words* of the specification, but not the *act of implementing it*. The patent promise above
follows the pattern the industry has settled on for open specifications — an irrevocable,
royalty-free non-assert with defensive termination — so that no reader of this repository has
to wonder whether building on the format exposes them to claims from its own authors, today or
after a change of stewardship.
